## Summary
💡 **What:** Added an `aria-label` attribute to the `SmartAssistButton` component.
🎯 **Why:** Icon-only buttons that use emojis (like ✨ and 🧠) are read literally by screen readers (e.g. "sparkles" or "brain") which lacks context. By adding an `aria-label` that mirrors the conditional `title` attribute, screen reader users get the exact same helpful text as mouse users ("Есть AI подсказки" or "Получить AI подсказки").
📸 **Before/After:** No visual changes.
♿ **Accessibility:** Fixes a critical WCAG violation for missing accessible names on icon-only interactive controls, dramatically improving the screen reader experience for the AI assist feature.
