## Summary
🎨 Palette: Add aria-hidden to Button loading spinner

💡 What: Added aria-hidden="true" to the loading spinner SVG in the Button component.
🎯 Why: To prevent redundant screen reader announcements when the button already has aria-busy.
📸 Before/After: Before, screen readers might read out the SVG. After, they ignore the visual spinner.
♿ Accessibility: Improved screen reader experience by hiding decorative loading elements.
